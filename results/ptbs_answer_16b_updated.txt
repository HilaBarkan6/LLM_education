\textbf{Central Data Structure:} AVL Tree. \textbf{Brief Description:} We will base our operations on the standard operations of an AVL tree, but change the insert and delete operations in order to improve the amortized delete complexity. \textbf{Explanation for Complexity:} In the banker's method - at the time of insertion \((x(\text{insert})\), we insert the element, and additionally leave \(\log n\) coins for its deletion thus making the delete operation \(O(\1)\) amortized
